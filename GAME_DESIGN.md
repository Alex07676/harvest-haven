# Harvest Haven game design

## Core promise

Harvest Haven is a shared, persistent valley where a one-hour play session has a clear rhythm: wake, choose a goal, travel through the town or farm, spend energy on meaningful work, and return with a visible change to the world.

It is inspired by the warmth and depth of classic farming-life RPGs, but uses original characters, art, map layout, story, and names.

## Time and progression

- An in-game day lasts 60 real minutes.
- Spring, summer, fall, and winter each last exactly seven real-world days.
- Crops grow in real minutes or hours, not in a single button press.
- Romance begins with a first conversation, requires at least 10 friendship, and cannot unlock before three real-world days have passed.
- Shops, festivals, crops, NPC schedules, and story chapters are designed around repeat visits rather than instant completion.

## World reasons

- The farm is the shared home and production space.
- Willowmere is the social and economic center.
- Juniper General sells seeds and supplies.
- Harvest Exchange buys gathered and grown goods.
- Silverrun River is the only fishing location and creates a reason to leave the farm.
- The seasonal festival is the payoff for the seven-day season and advances the Lanterns of Willowmere story.

## Relationships

The six romance routes are Mara, Elin, Signe, Freja, Liv, and Astrid. Each has a distinct appearance, style, daily location, favorite gift, and future story arc. Friendship comes from repeated conversations and thoughtful gifts. Romance is a progression milestone, not a purchase or a single interaction.

## Multiplayer ownership

Everyone shares one persistent farm and town. Each farmer owns their home, yard, inventory, coins, crops, and relationships. Other players can visit homes and cooperate, but owner-only crops and personal inventories prevent griefing and accidental theft.

## Interaction language

- WASD or arrow keys move.
- `1` through `8` select the hotbar.
- `Space` uses the selected tool.
- `E` interacts with nearby NPCs, shops, sell points, and the river.
- `B` buys seeds at Juniper General.
- `F` fishes only beside Silverrun.
- `T` trades at Harvest Exchange.

Touch controls remain available as a secondary tablet input, but the primary experience is keyboard, mouse, proximity, and contextual interaction.
